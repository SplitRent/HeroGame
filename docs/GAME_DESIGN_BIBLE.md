# Game Design Bible

**Working title:** SECOND LIFE (placeholder — trademark conflict, see TDD §0) · **Setting:** Port Arden, Tarrow County, Texas · **Doc version:** 0.1

> *"This isn't a game world waiting for me. This city exists, and I'm one person living inside it."*

This bible is the canonical reference for world, tone, characters and system intent. Server owners can rename and
re-brand almost everything (GDD §8, §178); Story Mode always uses the canon below.

---

## 1. Pillars

1. **A city that doesn't need you.** Every system runs without the player: NPCs work, marry, lose jobs; businesses
   trade; elections happen; storms arrive. The player is one life among millions.
2. **Any life is a valid game.** Ordinary citizen, entrepreneur, criminal, cop, parent, drifter, hero, villain — or
   several over one character's life. No mandatory loop.
3. **Consequences flow through systems, not scripts.** A storm closes a bar; the bar misses payroll; the bartender
   gets laid off; her rent goes unpaid; she takes a job from a crew that robs a pharmacy; the robbery makes the news.
4. **The extraordinary is rare.** Superpowers are an anomaly layered on a functioning modern society. Most players
   will never have one; everyone will live in a world that reacts to them.
5. **Believable over spectacular.** Simplify internally; never let the world look fake (GDD §184).

## 2. The city: Port Arden

### 2.1 Identity
| | |
|---|---|
| Official name | City of Port Arden, seat of Tarrow County, Texas |
| Metro population (canon) | ~2.9 million |
| Motto / slogan | "Built on the Tide" |
| Colours | Harbour blue `#1B4F72`, sodium amber `#F0B429` |
| Climate | humid subtropical; hot wet summers, mild winters, hurricane season June–November |
| Economy | petrochemicals, the port, logistics, medicine, energy trading, tourism on Coquina Key |
| Nicknames | "the Tide City", "PA", "Arden" |

### 2.2 History (canon timeline)
* **1839** — Founded by river traders at the mouth of **Calloway Bayou**; cotton wharves along what is now Cotton Row.
* **1867** — Rail reaches the wharves; Eastwater grows as housing for dock and rail workers.
* **1914** — The **Arden Ship Canal** is dredged inland, making Port Arden a deep-water port.
* **1921** — Oil. **Calloway Chemical Works** opens on the canal; Channelside industrialises.
* **1932** — The **Storm of '32** flattens Coquina Key and floods Eastwater; the seawall and the bayou levees follow.
* **1958** — The Tarrow Freeway cuts through Eastwater, splitting the neighbourhood.
* **1976** — Tarrow State University becomes a major research university (Halvorsen Institute of Physical Sciences).
* **1994** — Port of Arden container terminal expansion; Harbor Row warehouses begin conversion to lofts.
* **2018** — Meridian Tower, the tallest building on the Gulf Coast, opens in Arden Central.
* **2026, September** — **Hurricane Isadora**. During landfall, dozens of people in Eastwater and Channelside see debris
  hang motionless in the eye of a storm cell: the first widely documented **resonance event**. (Story Mode teen finale.)
* **2026–2030** — Rebuilding. Meridian Holdings buys flood-damaged land. Rare, unexplained incidents continue across the
  Gulf Coast; the phenomenon becomes a public debate. The federal **Coastal Anomaly Research Directorate (CARD)** opens a
  field office at the Port.
* **2030, May** — Story Mode adult era and the default server start date.

### 2.4 Landmarks (GDD §159)
Meridian Tower (skyline) · The Breakwater (Surge stadium) · Port of Arden terminal · Coquina Key Boardwalk & Pleasure
Pier · **Tidewater Bridge** (cable-stayed span over the ship canal) · Founders' Square & City Hall · **Arden General
Hospital** · Tarrow State University & the Halvorsen Institute · Westmarch Commons · **Cotton Row** · **Calloway Chemical
Works** (flare stacks visible across the city at night).

### 2.5 Art direction (GDD §157)
Contemporary, humid, coastal, sprawling, lived-in. Heat haze, wet asphalt after afternoon storms, live oaks and Spanish
moss in old neighbourhoods, sodium-orange refinery glow at night, pastel stucco on the Key, faded brick on Cotton Row.
Wealth reads through maintenance, landscaping and newness, never through caricature: Eastwater has porches, murals,
gardens and pride alongside peeling paint. No sterile futurism.

## 3. Institutions and organisations (all fictional)

### 3.1 Civic
* **City of Port Arden** — strong-mayor system; Mayor + 11 district council members; **elections are nonpartisan**
  (as in real Texas cities), contested through civic slates, not national parties.
* **Port Arden Police Department (PAPD)** — precincts per district; later the **Special Incident Response Unit (SIRU)**
  emerges in response to anomalies (only after enough incidents — GDD §108).
* **Port Arden Fire Rescue (PAFR)** — fire and EMS; Station 7 on Harbor Row.
* **Tarrow County Sheriff's Office**, **Port of Arden Authority**, **Tarrow County District Court**.
* Hospitals: **Arden General Hospital** (public, trauma centre), **St. Brigid Medical Center** (private).
* Schools: **Eastwater High School** (Pelicans), Pinecrest Hollow High, Coquina Key Academy; universities:
  **Tarrow State University** (Tarpons), **Arden Institute of Technology**.

### 3.2 Civic slates and issues (GDD §26–27)
| Slate | Platform |
|---|---|
| **Arden Renewal** | Flood control bonds, port expansion, "responsible development", anomaly registration ordinance |
| **Keep Arden Working** | Neighbourhood protections, anti-displacement, policing reform, oppose registration |
| Independents | Single-issue candidates (property tax, bayou restoration, stadium funding) |

Recurring issues: flood control, the Tarrow Freeway expansion, property taxes after Isadora, port pollution, police
budgets, and whether people with anomalous abilities must register. The game never takes sides; systems model
consequences (a registration ordinance changes police behaviour, public fear and powered players' options).

### 3.3 Economy & media
Gulf Tidewater Bank · Calloway Chemical · Meridian Holdings (real estate/development) · Bayline Logistics · Lopez
Brothers Construction · Tidewater Mercado (grocery chain) · **Channel 9 Gulfwatch** (TV news) · **The Arden Ledger**
(newspaper) · **KTDW Tidewater Radio** · **Ripple** (the in-world social platform, GDD §51).

### 3.5 Anomaly bodies
* **CARD** (Coastal Anomaly Research Directorate) — federal, secretive, collects "resonance signatures".
* **Halvorsen Institute** (Tarrow State) — public research, publishes the term *resonance*.
* Street/press slang for people with abilities: *resonants*, "rezzies" (pejorative), "anomalies".

### 3.6 Crime (never ethnic caricature)
Crime is mostly **emergent**: NPCs with financial stress and low agreeableness, player crews, opportunists during
storms. Authored organisations are defined by what they do:
* **The Tidewater Syndicate** — old-money organised crime laundering through port logistics and real estate.
* **The Night Market** — fencing network for stolen goods, moves between warehouses.
* Neighbourhood crews are player-formed or emergent, never pre-assigned to a district's ethnicity or income.

## 4. Superpower phenomenon — canon rules

* **Resonance events** are brief, localised distortions — lightning that strikes the same spot 30 times, fog that makes
  plants bloom overnight, debris frozen in the air. Most exposed people feel nothing. A few develop an ability days later.
* Nobody chooses a power. The event's *signature* biases what appears (electrical events → energy, pressure events →
  force/gravity). Body-level abilities (strength, durability, speed, healing) are common *among the rare*; manipulation
  of gravity, matter, space and time is vanishingly rare; a second ability is a once-in-a-city anomaly.
* Abilities arrive confusingly: symptoms first (lights flicker around you, you crush a door handle), control later.
* Society reacts gradually: rumours on Ripple → news coverage → public debate → insurance exclusions → the SIRU → a
  registration ordinance fight. Most powered people stay hidden.
* The in-world science is incomplete by design. The story reveals *some* truth (CARD knows events can be provoked),
  never a tidy origin that kills mystery.

## 5. Characters (Story Mode canon)

### 5.1 Protagonist — **Eli Navarro**
16 at the start (2026), 20 after the time jump. Grew up on Magnolia Street in Eastwater. Quick, restless, loyal,
funny under pressure, allergic to being told what he is. Wants: to keep his family afloat and to matter somewhere beyond
Eastwater. Fears: becoming his cousin Rafa — or becoming someone who left. Voice: bilingual, understated, dry.
Story Mode uses this authored protagonist (voiced, cinematic). Servers use the player's own created character.

### 5.2 Family and neighbourhood
* **Rosa Navarro** (44) — ER nurse at Arden General, night shifts. Tired, sharp, unbreakable; hides how close to broke they are.
* **Pilar Navarro** (9 → 13) — Eli's sister; science-obsessed; notices everything (she is the first to suspect Eli's secret).
* **Tomás Navarro** (deceased) — longshoreman, killed in a 2022 Calloway Chemical canal fire; the unresolved settlement
  is a thread through the story.
* **Rafa Navarro** (22 → 26) — cousin. Charming, reckless, runs small scams, drifts toward the Night Market.
* **Lupe Salazar** (67) — owns Lupe's Corner Market; Eli's first boss; the neighbourhood's memory and conscience.

### 5.3 Friends
* **Darnell "Dee" Okafor** (16) — Eli's best friend. Engineer brain, wants out; after the time jump, a Tarrow State
  grad student at the Halvorsen Institute measuring resonance events.
* **Mai Pham** (16) — sharp, stubborn, runs the school paper; later a reporter at The Arden Ledger investigating the
  land deals after Isadora. Possible romance, never mandatory.
* **Coach Harold Dupree** — Eastwater Pelicans football coach; believes in Eli more than Eli does.

### 5.4 Power and institutions
* **Councilwoman Yolanda Hollins** — Eastwater's council member; runs for mayor on the Keep Arden Working slate.
* **Grant Calloway** (38) — heir to Calloway Chemical, board member of Meridian Holdings. Not a cartoon: believes
  Port Arden dies without redevelopment, and that people like the Navarros are acceptable costs.
* **Detective Ruth Abernathy** (51) — PAPD, later SIRU commander. Methodical, fair, relentless. Will investigate Eli.
* **Dr. Anjali Rao** — CARD field director; knows more than she says.

## 6. Story Mode structure (GDD §55–60)

### Part One — "Magnolia Street" (teenage, 2026, linear-open neighbourhood)
1. **Mornings** — family, Magnolia Street, walking to school; learn movement and interaction.
2. **Eastwater High** — classes, lunch-table politics, Mai's paper, Dee's schemes; social systems introduced.
3. **Friday Lights** — the Pelicans game; sports minigame; the whole neighbourhood in one place.
4. **First Paycheck** — job at Lupe's; the career/work loop; money is tight at home.
5. **Rafa's Favor** — a "simple" delivery for Rafa; first encounter with crime, witnesses and police. Player choice
   with real consequences (Lupe's trust, Rafa's arrest, Rosa finding out).
6. **The Canal Lights** — a night at the ship canal with Dee and Mai; a strange lightning strike. A glimpse, nothing more.
7. **Isadora** — hurricane set piece: evacuation, flooding on Magnolia Street, rescuing neighbours. In the eye of the storm,
   debris hangs in the air around Eli. Nothing happens to him… apparently.

**FOUR YEARS LATER** (GDD §57) — cinematic transition showing the rebuilt city, Meridian's cranes over Eastwater, new
businesses, closed ones, Pilar grown up, Lupe older, Dee at Tarrow State, Mai at the Ledger, Rafa out of prison.

### Part Two — adult sandbox (2030, full city opens, GDD §58)
* **Act I "Back on Magnolia"** — Eli, 20, returns from two years on offshore platforms. Find a job, an apartment,
  reconnect. The open world, careers, property and business systems open.
* **Act II "Resonance"** — four years of latency end: Eli's ability manifests (Story Mode fixes the ability family per
  playthrough seed; servers never do). Hide it, test it, tell someone, or use it.
* **Act III "The Ledger"** — Mai's investigation: Meridian bought flooded Eastwater lots through shells; CARD records
  show Calloway's canal experiments preceded Isadora's event. The Tidewater Syndicate launders the money.
* **Act IV "Election Season"** — Hollins vs. the Arden Renewal candidate; the registration ordinance; Abernathy closing in.
* **Finale "Landfall"** — a new storm and a provoked resonance event at Calloway Works. Eli decides who he is in public.

Endings are shaped by identity exposure, alliances (Hollins, Abernathy, Rafa, Calloway), the election, and whether
Eastwater is saved, sold or split. After credits the sandbox continues; the world remembers the ending.

Story Mode missions avoid repetitive structure: each mission combines at least two systems (e.g. business + weather,
crime + family) and can be approached several ways.

## 7. Server (multiplayer) design

* Each server is its own world with its own history (GDD §99): same generator, different seed ⇒ different people,
  different businesses thriving, different storms.
* Players bring their global identity (face, name) and start their server life with modest cash and nothing else.
* Server owners customise identity (city name, colours, teams, orgs), gameplay (PvP, police, density, day length, death
  rules), economy (taxes, prices, wages, payouts — within integrity bounds) and powers (frequency, manifestation,
  multiple-power multiplier ≤5×, max powers ≤3).
* Canonical server browser filters: friends, region, population, ping, roleplay, PvP, hardcore, powers, economy,
  official/community, locked/whitelisted, version compatibility.

## 8. Core loops (none mandatory)

**Explore → interact → decide → build a life → experience consequences → discover opportunities → change your life.**

| Loop | Systems it touches |
|---|---|
| Work & career | jobs, schedules, performance, promotion, wages, reputation (professional) |
| Business | property, staff (NPCs), pricing, inventory, weather, economy, taxes, crime (robberies, fronts) |
| Property & home | purchase/rent, mortgage, furniture, building, security, damage, insurance |
| Social & family | relationships, memory, households, events, secret identity |
| Crime | witnesses, evidence, wanted, police, courts, notoriety, fences |
| Powers | anomaly exposure, discovery, practice, secret identity, public reaction, SIRU |
| Civic | elections, ordinances, taxes, public services |
| Leisure | sports, restaurants, nightlife, shopping, beach, fishing, racing, exploring |

## 9. Tone and content boundaries

* Grounded, humane, occasionally funny, occasionally tragic. Never cruel toward poverty or neighbourhoods.
* Violence exists in the crime/action layer with non-lethal options and real consequences; no sexual violence; no
  real-world hate groups; real-world partisan politics are never referenced.
* Weapons are original designs; vehicles are original designs; music is original or licensed.
